《采光日照计》隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日　|　最近更新：2026 年 9 月 26 日　|　适用版本：1.0 及以后

一句话总结：《采光日照计》不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何个人信息。它是一个完全在你自己手机上离线运行的计算工具，没有账号、没有服务器、没有广告、没有数据分析。

一、我们是谁

本 App《采光日照计》（英文名 Daylight & Sunshine Meter，副标题"住宅光照分析专家"，以下简称"本 App"）由独立开发者杨亚丽开发并运营。如你对本政策有任何疑问，可通过 sursor@163.com 联系我们。

二、我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。具体而言：

常见数据类型本 App 是否涉及
姓名、手机号、邮箱、账号密码不涉及。本 App 没有账号系统，无需注册登录。
位置信息仅在你主动点击"使用当前位置"时，读取当前经纬度，只在本机内存与本地文件中用于计算，绝不上传。
设备标识符（IDFA、IDFV 等）不涉及。
使用行为、崩溃日志、分析数据不涉及。本 App 未集成任何统计、崩溃上报或广告 SDK。
通讯录、照片、麦克风、相机内容不涉及。仅在保存报告长图时向系统相册写入图片，不读取你已有的照片。
支付信息不涉及。付款由 Apple 处理，我们从不接触你的银行卡号或支付凭证。

三、系统权限说明

本 App 只申请两项权限，且都在你使用到对应功能时才会弹出询问：

权限用途拒绝的后果
定位（仅使用期间）用于自动确定你的测算地点，以便按当地经纬度计算太阳位置与日照时长。若你选择“使用当前位置”，App 会调用 iOS 系统自带的定位与地名反查服务来显示地点名称，这一步由 Apple 处理并遵循 Apple 的隐私政策；除此之外，经纬度仅在本机参与计算。可正常使用，改为手动搜索并选择城市。
照片（仅添加）用于把全年采光报告的长图保存到你的相册。可正常使用，仅无法保存长图。

本 App 不申请后台定位、通讯录、相机、麦克风、健康数据等任何其它权限，也不启用后台运行模式。

四、信息在什么位置存储

你的测算记录与偏好设置全部保存在你自己的设备内部，不会离开设备：

  • 测算记录：保存在 App 沙盒内的数据文件中，包含你选择的地点名称与经纬度、月份、高度、楼距、角度等参数，以及该次计算的结果。你付费生成的报告不会被自动清理，会一直保留至你自行删除；免费试用产生的记录最多保留 100 条。
  • 偏好设置：保存在系统本地偏好存储中，包括首次引导是否已读、免费试用是否已使用、内购状态缓存、最近一次使用的城市。其中“免费试用是否已使用”这一标记还会同时写入 iOS 系统钥匙串（Keychain），目的是让它在删除并重新安装 App 后依然有效，即每台设备只能享受一次免费试用。该标记只是一个不含任何个人信息的布尔值，也不会离开你的设备。
  • 城市数据：中国县级行政区列表已随 App 一起打包，不联网获取。

本 App 不进行任何网络请求来上传或同步上述内容，也不提供云备份功能。

五、应用内购买

本 App 提供消耗型 App 内购买项目：每发起一次新的测算需付费一次，付费后生成一份全年采光报告并支持导出长图；已生成的报告可无限次重新打开与重新导出，不再收费。全部支付流程由 Apple 通过 App Store 完成，遵循 Apple 的隐私政策。我们收到的仅是 Apple 提供的不含个人身份的购买凭据（用于判断是否已购买），不包含你的姓名、支付账号或账单地址。

六、第三方组件与追踪

本 App 未集成任何第三方 SDK，不使用 Cookie 或类似追踪技术，不进行跨 App 或跨网站的追踪，也不投放任何广告。

七、儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。由于本 App 不收集任何个人信息，儿童使用本 App 亦不会产生个人信息收集。

八、你的选择与数据删除

  • 你可以随时在 App 内删除单条测算记录。
  • 你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 定位服务 / 照片」中收回已授予的权限。
  • 删除 App 会彻底删除本机全部测算记录与偏好设置，唯一例外是钥匙串中“免费试用已使用”这个布尔标记（用于防止反复重装规避试用限制，不含任何个人信息）。因为我们不持有你的任何数据副本，故无需另行提出删除请求。
  • 若你所在地区的法律赋予你访问、更正、删除个人数据或数据可携的权利：由于我们不收集可识别你身份的个人信息，我们客观上无从定位属于你的数据；你可以通过上述方式直接在本机完成全部删除。

九、数据安全与跨境传输

由于本 App 不收集、不上传任何个人信息，不存在个人信息在服务器端存储、泄露或被跨境传输的情形。本机数据受 iOS 系统沙盒与应用加密机制保护。

十、关于测算结果的免责声明

本 App 的采光与日照时长结果，基于太阳位置天文算法与简化的几何遮挡模型估算，用于帮助你对比不同房源的采光条件。测算结果仅供参考，不构成对采光、日照时长或房产价值的任何法律、交易或专业承诺。实际采光状况还会受到天气、地形、周边建筑变化、玻璃透过率与大气散射等因素影响，请以现场实测为准。本 App 的日常测算结果不适用于需要遵循国家或地区建筑采光标准的正式评定用途。

十一、本政策的变更

如本政策发生实质性变更，我们会在本页面更新并同步修改"最近更新"日期；涉及新增数据处理的变更，还会在 App 内以显著方式提示。建议你定期查阅本页面。

对本政策或本 App 的隐私实践有任何问题、意见或投诉，请联系：

Daylight & Sunshine Meter Privacy Policy

Effective: September 26, 2026　|　Last updated: September 26, 2026　|　Applies to version 1.0 and later

In one sentence: Daylight & Sunshine Meter does not collect, upload, or share any of your personal information. It is a fully offline calculator that runs entirely on your own iPhone — no account, no server, no ads, no analytics.

1. Who we are

Daylight & Sunshine Meter (Chinese name 采光日照计, subtitle "Sunlight Hours Calculator", the "App") is developed and operated by Yang Yali, an independent developer. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at sursor@163.com.

2. What we collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically:

Common data typeHandled by this App?
Name, phone number, email, account credentialsNo. The App has no account system and requires no sign-in.
LocationOnly when you tap "Use current location" does the App read your coordinates — used on your device only for the sunlight calculation. Never uploaded.
Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, etc.)No.
Usage behaviour, crash logs, analyticsNo. No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK is integrated.
Contacts, Photos, Microphone, Camera contentNo. When saving a report image the App only writes to your photo library; it never reads your existing photos.
Payment informationNo. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your card number or payment credentials.

3. System permissions

The App requests only two permissions, and each is asked only when you use the related feature:

PermissionPurposeIf you decline
Location (While Using)To determine your measurement location automatically, so sunlight can be computed for your coordinates. If you choose Use current location, the app calls the iOS built-in location and reverse-geocoding services to show a place name — that step is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. Otherwise the coordinates are used on-device only.The App still works — search and pick a city manually.
Photos (Add Only)To save the annual daylight report image to your photo library.The App still works — only image saving is unavailable.

The App requests no background location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health permissions, and uses no background modes.

4. Where your information is stored

Your measurements and preferences are stored entirely inside your own device and never leave it:

  • Measurements — stored in a data file within the App sandbox: the place name and coordinates, month, heights, distance, angles, and the computed result. Reports you paid for are never cleaned up automatically and stay until you delete them; free-trial records are capped at 100.
  • Preferences — stored in the system's on-device preferences: onboarding completed, whether the free trial has been used, cached purchase state, and last used city. The free-trial-used flag is additionally written to the iOS Keychain so that it survives deleting and reinstalling the app — that is what enforces one free trial per device. It is a boolean containing no personal information, and it never leaves your device.
  • City data — the list of Chinese county-level districts ships inside the App; nothing is fetched online.

No network request is made to upload or sync any of the above, and there is no cloud backup.

5. In-app purchases

The App offers a consumable in-app purchase: each brand-new measurement requires one purchase, which unlocks the generation of one annual daylight report with image export. Reports already generated can be reopened and re-exported without further charge. All payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple's privacy policy. We receive only a non-identifying purchase receipt from Apple (used to determine whether a purchase exists) — never your name, payment account, or billing address.

6. Third parties and tracking

The App integrates no third-party SDKs, uses no cookies or similar tracking technologies, performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and displays no advertising.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the App collects no personal information at all, no personal information is collected when a child uses it.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • You can delete any measurement record inside the App at any time.
  • You can revoke granted permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services / Photos.
  • Deleting the App erases every measurement and preference on the device. The one exception is the Keychain flag recording that the free trial has been used — a boolean with no personal information, kept so that reinstalling cannot reset the trial. Because we hold no copy, no separate deletion request is needed.
  • If the law of your region grants you rights of access, correction, erasure, or portability: since we collect no personally identifiable information, we have no way to locate data belonging to you; you can complete the full erasure yourself using the steps above.

9. Data security and international transfers

Because the App collects and uploads no personal information, there is no server-side storage, disclosure, or cross-border transfer of personal information. On-device data is protected by the iOS sandbox and system encryption.

10. Disclaimer regarding results

The daylight and sunlight-duration results are estimated using standard solar-position algorithms and a simplified geometric obstruction model, to help you compare daylight conditions between properties. Results are for reference only and do not constitute any legal, transactional, or professional commitment regarding daylight, sunlight duration, or property value. Actual conditions also depend on weather, terrain, changes to surrounding buildings, glazing transmittance, and atmospheric scattering — please rely on on-site measurement. The App's everyday results are not intended for formal assessment purposes that must follow national or regional building daylight standards.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date; changes that introduce new data handling will also be highlighted in the App. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

12. Contact us

For any question, comment, or complaint about this policy or the App's privacy practices, contact:
Email: sursor@163.com
We will respond within 15 business days.
