关于收费

免费能用吗？免费试用的范围是什么？

可以。每台设备都享有一次免费试用机会，试用范围是当前自然月：你会得到所选地点当月的月均每日日照时长、每日明细和当天的阳光时段。

免费试用按设备计算：每台设备只有一次，用完即止，删除 App 后重新安装也不会再次获得。试用时只能测算当前自然月，不能自选其他月份；也不包含全年 12 个月报告，不支持导出长图。

我删掉 App 重装后，为什么免费试用没有了？

免费试用是按设备计算的，每台设备只能享受一次。该标记除了保存在 App 本地，还会写入 iOS 系统钥匙串（Keychain），因此删除并重新安装 App 不会重置试用机会。这样做是为了防止通过反复重装无限次免费测算。若你确实遇到了异常（例如换了新手机但试用机会已用），请发邮件到 sursor@163.com 说明情况。

付费一次能得到什么？

付费一次相当于购买一份完整的全年采光报告，包含：

  • 所选地点全年 12 个月的月均每日日照时长与逐月对比柱状图
  • 每月每日的日照明细数据
  • 采光总结与分档结论
  • 报告长图导出并保存到相册，可随时分享给家人或中介
  • 该份报告永久保存在你的历史记录中，可随时重新打开查看，也可以不额外付费再次导出长图
我是按年订阅吗？会自动续费吗？

不是订阅，不会自动续费，不存在到期扣款。但请注意本 App 是按次付费：每发起一次新的测算就需要付费一次；而已经生成并保存在历史记录里的报告，反复打开、反复导出长图都不会再扣款。每次付款金额固定、可预期，具体价格以 App 内购买页显示的金额为准（各国家或地区价格由 App Store 按当地价格展示）。

为什么每算一次都要付费？

之所以按次收费，是因为每一次测算都是一份独立的、针对特定房源与参数生成的完整报告，可以单独保存、单独分享。免费试用让你先看到单月报告的实际样子，再决定要不要为全年报告付费。

报告看过了，再打开还要付费吗？

不需要。已经生成并保存在历史记录里的报告属于你，可以无限次重新打开、重新导出长图，不再收取任何费用。只有新生成一份报告时才需要再次购买。

我改了一个参数想重算，要再付一次吗？

修改参数重新测算会产生一份新的报告，因此需要再次购买。建议在点击计算前，先在确认页核对地点、月份、高度、楼距与角度是否填对。

付款成功但没看到报告，怎么办？

请先按以下顺序排查：

  1. 返回首页，查看历史记录列表最上方是否已新增一条记录（报告会立即保存）。
  2. 完全退出 App（从后台向上划掉）后重新打开，App 会自动补发未完成的购买。
  3. 仍然没有的话，请重启手机后再打开 App 一次——启动时 App 会自动向 App Store 校验并补发未完成的交易。
  4. 以上都无效，请把 Apple 发给你的购买收据邮件转发到 sursor@163.com，我们会为你处理。
换了新 iPhone，之前买的报告还能看吗？

已生成的报告保存在原设备本地。如果你用同一 Apple ID 在 iPhone 之间做了整机迁移或从备份恢复，历史记录会一并带过去。由于本 App 不设账号也不上传数据，若你没有做备份，原设备上未迁移的记录无法找回，请留意。如果你已完成付款却没有生成报告，请把 Apple 发给你的购买收据邮件转发到 sursor@163.com，我们会协助你补发。

关于测算

结果准不准？为什么和我的实际感受不一样？

结果基于太阳位置天文算法与简化的几何遮挡模型估算，用于对比不同房源的采光条件，属于参考性质而非实测值。实际采光还会受到天气、玻璃透过率、大气散射、周边建筑后续变化等因素影响，请以现场实拍与实测为准。

我需要准备哪些数据？难不难填？

一共只需要几个数：地点、月份、遮挡物与窗户的相对高差、楼距、以及遮挡物张开的角度。App 内有逐步引导和字段说明，卡在任何一步都可以点击该字段旁的 ⓘ 查看图文讲解。楼层数只是辅助估算高度的快捷方式，不是必填。

遮挡物在房子北边，或者角度超过 90°，能算吗？

本工具只评估朝向默认采光方向（北半球为南向、南半球为北向）±90° 范围内的遮挡对采光的影响。位于房屋北侧、或东西两侧超出该范围的遮挡物不在本模型范围内，可视为无遮挡。这是模型边界，敬请留意。

支持哪些地区？

本 App 内置中国县级行政区数据（含中国香港、中国澳门），在中国大陆及港澳可直接搜索并选择城市。其他国家和地区的用户请使用「使用当前位置」：App 会读取当前经纬度并进行全球地名反查，反查不到时会直接以坐标显示并照常计算。

本 App 的太阳位置算法本身适用于全球任意纬度，南半球会自动切换默认采光方向。

关于数据与隐私

我的位置和测算数据会被上传吗？

不会。所有测算记录与设置都保存在你的手机本地，App 不联网上传任何数据，没有账号系统，也没有服务器。详见《隐私政策》。

历史记录最多能存多少条？

你付费生成的报告不会被自动清理，会一直保留到你自己删除。免费试用产生的记录最多保留 100 条，超出后会提示你清理。你可以在首页左滑删除任意一条记录，但请留意：付费报告删除后需要重新付费才能再次生成。

怎么删除全部数据？

删除 App 即可彻底清除全部本机数据。也可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性」中收回定位或照片权限。

Purchases

Is there a free version? What does the free trial cover?

Yes. Each device gets one free trial, covering the current calendar month: you get the monthly average daily sunlight for your chosen location, the per-day breakdown, and the sunny time windows for the typical day.

The free trial is counted per device: one trial per device, gone once used, and deleting then reinstalling the app does not grant another one. The trial only covers the current calendar month and cannot be pointed at any other month. It does not include the 12-month annual report and does not support image export.

I deleted and reinstalled the app — why is my free trial gone?

The free trial is counted per device and is granted only once. The flag is kept both in the app and in the iOS Keychain, so deleting and reinstalling the app does not reset it. This exists to prevent unlimited free measurements through repeated reinstalls. If you hit a genuine problem (for example, you moved to a new phone but the trial already counts as used), email sursor@163.com and tell us what happened.

What do I get for one purchase?

One purchase buys one complete annual daylight report, including:

  • Monthly average daily sunlight for all 12 months, with a comparison chart
  • Per-day detail for every month
  • A daylight summary with a graded conclusion
  • Report image export saved to your photo library, ready to share
  • The report stays in your history forever — reopen and re-export it any time at no extra cost
Is this a subscription? Will I be charged automatically?

No subscription and no auto-renewal — nothing is ever charged just because time passes. But note how this app charges: it is pay-per-calculation. Every brand-new measurement requires a new purchase, while reports already saved in your history can be reopened and re-exported as many times as you like at no extra cost. Each charge is a fixed, predictable amount shown on the in-app purchase screen (App Store displays it in your local currency).

Why do I pay per calculation?

Because each measurement produces a separate, complete report for one specific property and parameter set, which you can save and share independently. The free trial lets you see what a real monthly report looks like before deciding whether to pay for the annual one.

Do I pay again to reopen a report I already bought?

No. Any report saved in your history is yours — reopen it and re-export the image as many times as you like, free of charge. You only pay again when you generate a brand-new report.

I changed a parameter and want to recalculate — another charge?

Yes — recalculating with new parameters generates a new report, so it requires a new purchase. Please double-check location, month, heights, distance, and angles on the confirmation step before tapping calculate.

I paid but can't see the report. What now?

Please try, in order:

  1. Go back to the home screen — the report is saved immediately, so check the top of your history list.
  2. Force-quit the app (swipe it away in the app switcher) and reopen it; unfinished purchases are re-delivered automatically.
  3. If it is still missing, restart your iPhone and open the app again — on launch it checks with the App Store and re-delivers any unfinished transaction.
  4. If none of the above helps, forward the Apple purchase receipt to sursor@163.com and we will sort it out.
I got a new iPhone — can I still see my old reports?

Generated reports live on the original device. If you migrate to a new iPhone with the same Apple ID, or restore from a backup, the history comes along. Because the app has no account and uploads nothing, any records not carried over by a backup cannot be recovered — please keep that in mind. If you were charged but no report was generated, forward the Apple purchase receipt to sursor@163.com and we will help you get it re-delivered.

How do I request a refund?

Apple collects all payments, so refunds must go through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the order. We cannot issue refunds on your behalf, but we can help confirm the order details.

Measurements

How accurate are the results?

Results are estimated with standard solar-position algorithms and a simplified geometric obstruction model, intended for comparing daylight conditions between properties — reference figures, not measured values. Real conditions also depend on weather, glazing transmittance, atmospheric scattering, and later changes to surrounding buildings. Please rely on on-site observation and measurement.

What data do I need to enter? Is it hard?

Just a few numbers: location, month, the relative height difference between the obstruction and your window, the distance, and the angle the obstruction spans. The app walks you through it step by step — tap the ⓘ next to any field for a visual explanation. Floor count is only a shortcut for estimating heights, not a required field.

What if the obstruction is to the north, or spans more than 90°?

This tool evaluates obstructions within ±90° of the default daylight direction (due south in the northern hemisphere, due north in the southern hemisphere). Obstructions to the north of the building, or beyond that arc to the east or west, fall outside the model and may be treated as unobstructed. Please note this model boundary.

Which regions are supported?

The app ships with county-level administrative data for China (including Hong Kong, China and Macao, China), so you can search and pick a city there directly. Elsewhere, use "Use current location": the app reads your coordinates and performs a worldwide reverse geocode; if that fails it simply displays the coordinates and calculates as usual.

The solar-position algorithm works at any latitude worldwide, and the southern hemisphere automatically flips the default daylight direction.

Data & privacy

Is my location or measurement data uploaded?

No. All measurements and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. The app uploads nothing, has no account system, and has no server. See the Privacy Policy.

How many records can I keep?

Reports you paid for are never cleaned up automatically — they stay until you delete them yourself. Free-trial records are capped at 100, and beyond that the app prompts you to tidy up. You can swipe left on any record to delete it, but note that a deleted paid report must be bought again to regenerate.

How do I erase all data?

Delete the app and all on-device data is gone. You can also revoke location or photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
